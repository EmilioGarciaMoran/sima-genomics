# sima-genomics

Ancient-DNA analyses of the Sima de los Huesos hominins (Atapuerca, ~430 ka).
Análisis de ADN antiguo de los homínidos de la Sima de los Huesos (Atapuerca, ~430 ka).

> **⚠ Correction (21 Oct 2026).** The mitochondrial conclusion of the April 2026 preprint (Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.19389351) and of the October 2026 manuscript is corrected. With explicit outgroup rooting, Sima is sister to Denisova, as in Meyer et al. (2014). **Read [`CORRECTION.md`](CORRECTION.md) first.**
>
> **The nuclear figures of those documents are withdrawn** (26 Oct 2026). No output supporting them was found, and a power study shows a Z of that order is unreachable with Sima's data volumes. They are replaced by the pre-registered analysis in [`nuclear_repro/`](nuclear_repro/), which confirms Meyer et al. 2016: Sima falls on the Neanderthal lineage, D = 0.586 ± 0.062, Z = 9.5.
>
> **⚠ Corrección (21 oct 2026).** La conclusión mitocondrial del preprint de abril de 2026 y del manuscrito de octubre de 2026 queda corregida: con raíz explícita en el grupo externo, Sima es hermano de Denisova, como en Meyer et al. (2014). Detalles en [`CORRECTION.md`](CORRECTION.md).
>
> **Las cifras nucleares de esos documentos quedan retiradas** (26 oct 2026) y sustituidas por el análisis preregistrado de [`nuclear_repro/`](nuclear_repro/), que confirma Meyer et al. 2016.

## Reproduce the nuclear analysis

```bash
cd nuclear_repro
cat README.md          # result, limitations and the order of the steps
```

The hypotheses, thresholds, controls and preprocessing parameters were fixed in
[`nuclear_repro/PREREGISTRO_D_statistics.md`](nuclear_repro/PREREGISTRO_D_statistics.md)
before any Sima read was mapped; later deviations carry their date and reason.

## Reproduce the mtDNA analysis

```bash
cd mtDNA_repro
micromamba create -f environment.yml && micromamba activate mtphylo
bash run_all.sh genbank 2      # ~16 min on 2 cores
git diff --stat -- sequences alignments results/CLADE_SUPPORT.tsv   # no output = reproduced
```
